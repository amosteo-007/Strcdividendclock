How the STRC dividend clock works

What STRC is

STRC is the ticker for Strategy's Variable Rate Series A Perpetual Stretch Preferred Stock, a preferred share with a $100 stated amount. It pays a dividend at a variable rate that Strategy reviews monthly, currently 12.00%. At $100 stated, that is about $12.00 per share per year, or roughly 3.3 cents per share per day.

How the daily payments work

Under a daily regime approved on 28 October 2026 and effective 1 November 2026, the dividend accrues every calendar day and is paid on the next business day. Dividends are cumulative, but they are payable only when declared by the board from legally available funds. The counters on this page assume continuous accrual, so they illustrate that accrual rather than show actual payment events.

What the tanks show

The blue tank is Strategy's USD assets: the USD Reserve plus USD Cash that the company reports. The orange tank is MSTR, Strategy's common stock, shown by market capitalization. Hover over a tank to see its amount. The leak at the bottom of the blue tank is STRC's dividend bill, dripping out at the rate shown in the headline.

Why the dollar tank looks small

Both tanks use the same scale, with a full tank equal to $60 billion, so the heights are directly comparable. MSTR's market value is roughly ten times Strategy's dollar holdings, so the blue tank looks like a puddle next to it. Strategy's bitcoin holdings are not in either tank.

What the pipe between the tanks means

Strategy's SEC filings show it raises cash by selling MSTR shares through an at-the-market (ATM) program, and part of that cash can go into the USD Reserve. The pipe is a symbol of that relationship. The animation is not a measured flow and its speed means nothing.

Where the numbers come from

USD Reserve and USD Cash come from Strategy's SEC filings or its public dashboard, whichever is newer, and the page states which source and date it is using. MSTR market cap and annual interest and dividends are copied from the dashboard and carry their own date. The accrual counters are calculations from a fixed share count and rate, not live data. In one weekly filing, for 28 September to 4 October 2026, Strategy reported using $142.5 million of the USD Reserve for preferred dividends and debt interest.

Frequently asked questions

Can Strategy's dollar reserve run out?

Strategy says its USD assets cover about 3.3 years of all interest and dividends at current obligations. That is a company-reported snapshot. It changes as the company pays dividends, repurchases stock, raises cash or issues new preferred shares. This page does not predict the reserve's future.

Is the STRC dividend guaranteed?

No. The rate is variable and reviewed monthly. Dividends are cumulative but payable only as declared by the board from legally available funds. STRC is an unsecured preferred security and is not backed by Strategy's bitcoin.

Are these numbers live?

Not tick by tick. The reserve and cash figures update when Strategy files or publishes new numbers, and this page shows the date it is using. The accrual counters run off your device's clock. Treat everything as illustrative and check the original sources before relying on any figure.

No. It is an unofficial, informational project and is not investment advice.
